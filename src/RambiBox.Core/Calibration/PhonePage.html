One-time setup for the live camera

The live camera needs a secure page. RambiBox made its own certificate for this PC; your phone needs to trust it once. It only works on your home network (private addresses) and can be removed any time in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

iPhone only installs certificate profiles from Safari. Open this setup in Safari:

Open in Safari

Or open Safari and type the address above. After the setup, come back to this browser — this page continues by itself.

1. Download the certificate profile

Tap “Allow” when Safari asks.

  1. Download the profile (in Safari) and tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded (near the top) → Install → enter your passcode → Install.
  3. Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → turn on full trust for “RambiBox Local CA”.
  4. Come back to this page. It switches to the live camera automatically.

No setup? Use a video instead

Record a ~25 second video of the screen while the PC shows a color loop. Works without any setup — the live camera is just more convenient.

  1. Turn off the room lights if you can.
  2. Stand 1.5–2 m in front of the screen so the whole screen and the glow around it are visible.
  3. Hold the phone steady. The PC changes colors by itself; just keep pointing at it.

Starting…

    Brave Shields add noise to camera images. For best accuracy turn off Shields for this page (lion icon in the address bar). Calibration continues anyway.

    Blue = screen reference, green = LED glow, red = not usable.

    Video calibration

    1. Turn off the room lights if you can.
    2. Tap Record video: the PC starts a color loop and your camera opens.
    3. Film the whole screen with the glow around it, hold still and record at least 25 seconds (the PC shows the loop time).
    4. Stop, then tap Use Video. The phone extracts frames and sends them to the PC.

    Tip: if the result looks off, set Settings → Camera → Record Video → HDR Video off.

    Look at the glow on the wall and the white edge of the screen, not at the phone. Let your eyes adapt for a few seconds, then drag the dot until both whites look the same.

    The screen edge and the LEDs show the same color. A = with the measured color correction, B = without. Tap A and B a few times and judge which glow matches the screen edge better.

    All done! The new LED colors are saved. You can close this page.
