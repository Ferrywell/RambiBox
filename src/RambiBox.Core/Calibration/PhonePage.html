One-time setup

The live camera needs a secure page. RambiBox made its own certificate for this PC; your phone needs to trust it once. It only works on your home network (private addresses) and can be removed any time in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

  1. Download the certificate profile
    Download profile

    Tap Allow when Safari asks.

    Your browser saves it as a download: open the Files app → Downloads (or the browser's downloads list) and tap RambiBox. If that doesn't work, open Safari and type
  2. Install it

    Open Settings → Profile Downloaded (near the top) → Install → enter your passcode → Install.

  3. Turn on full trust

    Important: Settings → General → About → scroll all the way down → Certificate Trust Settings → turn on “RambiBox Local CA”. Installing alone is not enough.

  4. Come back here

    The page continues by itself — or tap the button below.

Live camera

The PC shows color patterns while your phone's camera compares the LED glow with the screen. Takes about two minutes.

  1. Dim the room

    Turn off the room lights if you can.

  2. Frame the whole screen

    Stand 1.5–2 m in front of the screen so the whole screen and some wall around it are visible. Portrait or landscape both work.

  3. Hold steady

    The PC changes colors and adjusts the screen brightness for your camera by itself; just keep pointing at it.

    Video calibration

    Works without any setup: film the screen while the PC plays a color loop.

    1. Dim the room

      Turn off the room lights if you can.

    2. Record

      Tap Record video: the PC starts a color loop and your camera opens.

    3. Film the screen and the glow

      Keep the whole screen with the glow around it in view, hold still and record at least 25 seconds (the PC shows the loop time).

    4. Use the video

      Stop, then tap Use Video. The phone extracts frames and sends them to the PC.

    Opening the video…

    Tip: if the result looks off, set Settings → Camera → Record Video → HDR Video off.
